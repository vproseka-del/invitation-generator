# 1. Purpose

This document defines the communication principles used when generating letters, invitations and related materials for the project «Физики и лирики».

The objective is to ensure that every communication supports the mission of the project and follows a consistent strategy.

---

# 2. Core Principle

The application does not simply generate letters.

It helps begin a professional dialogue.

Every message should encourage cooperation rather than merely request an action.

---

# 3. Primary Goal of the First Letter

The first communication should:

- explain why the project exists;
- explain why the recipient is important;
- establish trust;
- motivate the recipient to complete the survey;
- open the door for future cooperation.

---

# 4. Communication Flow

1. Introduce the context.
2. Explain the project's mission.
3. Explain why this recipient is being contacted.
4. Describe the expected contribution.
5. Invite participation in the survey.
6. Thank the recipient.
7. Leave the door open for continued dialogue.

---

# 5. Communication Principles

Every letter must be:

- respectful;
- clear;
- concise;
- well-structured;
- fact-based;
- sincere.

Avoid pressure, exaggeration and unnecessary emotional language.

---

# 6. Anticipate Questions

Generated letters should naturally answer the recipient's likely questions:

- Why is this project needed?
- Why now?
- Why us?
- What is expected?
- What will happen after the survey?

These answers should be integrated into the text whenever appropriate.

---

# 7. Letter Structure

Recommended order:

1. Greeting.
2. Reason for the letter.
3. Project mission.
4. Why the recipient matters.
5. Requested action.
6. Survey.
7. Gratitude.
8. Contact information.

---

# 8. Survey Integration

The survey is not a separate activity.

The letter should naturally lead the recipient to participate.

Letters and surveys form one communication package.

---

# 9. Personalization

Personalization is more than replacing names.

Whenever possible, adapt the message to:

- organization type;
- recipient role;
- expected contribution;
- communication context.

---

# 10. Success Criteria

A communication is successful if, after reading it, the recipient:

- understands the project's purpose;
- understands why they were contacted;
- trusts the organizers;
- is willing to complete the survey;
- is open to future cooperation.

---

# 11. Core Principle

Every communication should answer one question:

> Why is it worth investing time in this project?

If the answer is not obvious, improve the communication before sending it.
